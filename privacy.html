Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Home Assistant (personal) is used only by its owner. When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives access to the owner's Nest devices through the Smart Device Management API (camera streams, snapshots, doorbell and motion events).

  • That data is used only to show and automate the owner's devices on their own home server.
  • It stays on that home server. It is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which stops all further access.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
